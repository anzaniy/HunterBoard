HOW TO READ THIS BOARD

Three things worth knowing before you compare rosters elsewhere.

SIZE

Large vs small

Large monsters are the hunt targets. Small monsters are the endemic wildlife you meet on the way — they share the roster but are not quest objectives.

RADIANT

What Radiant means

Radiant species are existing monsters changed by Radiantite. Their appearance and behaviour differ from the base species; they are not simple recolours with higher stats.

COUNTS

Why totals disagree

Trackers count differently: some list only huntable large monsters, others include small monsters and datamined entries. This board follows the wiki roster and labels upcoming entries instead of guessing.

! No leak data on this page. Entries come from the public roster maintained by the Monster Hunter Wiki and Game8’s launch coverage. If a monster has no article yet, the board says so rather than filling the gap.
