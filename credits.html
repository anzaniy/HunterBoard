All monster and weapon icons are stored locally under wilds/assets/. Apart from that, the only runtime external request is Google Fonts. No analytics, trackers or CDN scripts are loaded.

Release date, platforms, device requirements, genre, Adventurer profiles, buddy types and Radiant monster descriptions.

monsterhunteroutlanders.com
OFFICIAL

Capcom press release

The 29 October 2026 launch announcement, TiMi Studio Group as developer, free-to-play pricing and the mainland China window.

PRIMARY

Monster Hunter Wiki 窶・Outlanders roster

Large and small monster list, Radiant status, first appearances and upcoming entries. The monster board's 59 confirmed monsters, 24 Radiant species and 40 / 19 size split come from this list, reviewed against the revision of 2 November 2026.

monsterhunterwiki.org
CROSS-CHECK

Game8 窶・All Monsters List

Huntable roster and launch ordering for large monsters. Game8 counts fewer entries than the wiki, so it is used to confirm names and order, not to set the totals.

game8.co
