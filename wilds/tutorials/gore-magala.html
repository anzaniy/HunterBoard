MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Gore Magala

Iceshard Cliffs · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A Demi Elder (juvenile Elder Dragon) built around the Frenzy Virus. It is fast, flies often, and punishes passive play: the correct response to infection is to attack harder, not to disengage. Breaking its horns is the lever that ends its powered-up state.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Frenzy Virus: a gauge builds; fill it and you are debuffed (reduced natural healing, more damage taken, red health gone). Land enough hits before it fills to "overcome" it and gain a temporary Affinity boost.
  • Nulberry shortens the gauge; Antivirus/Frenzy Resistance skills ease the check.
  • Frenzied/Demon state: horns extend and glow purple, it moves faster and hits harder; a Strong Roar announces entry.
  • It contaminates areas with Frenzied air — lingering there infects you.
  • Horns can only be broken while they are out in that state; breaking them is the fastest way out of it.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Dive bomb from flight onto the hunter's position.
  • Charge that crosses between targets (flinches melee).
  • Wing-arm slams, tail swipes, Frenzy projectiles and horizontal/ring AoE bursts.
  • Roar into immediate follow-up while enraged.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Stay close and keep hitting through the virus; the Affinity reward outvalues retreating.
  • Attack the head/feelers when they extend; use traps for a horn-break window.
  • Feet and rear are safer damage spots, but the tail counter-swings if you camp behind it.
  • Traps work, including during the Frenzied state.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fire is the primary weakness in most guides; Thunder and Dragon are also listed as effective. Water is resisted and Waterblight is nullified. Blast and Exhaust are noted as effective.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, feelers/antennae (only when extended), wings; tail severable.

Recommended Skills & Items

Nulberry, Antivirus or Frenzy Resistance, Coalescence, Resuscitate, traps. Sources conflict on Flash Pods (one lists it as an immunity, another recommends it) — treat Flash as unreliable.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Base-game monster; 9★ Tempered available from Ver. 1.021 (Aug 2025). No Arch-Tempered form.
