Guardian Doshaguma

Ruins of Wyveria

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · MEDIUM confidence

Guardian Doshaguma is the artificial Doshaguma guardian, slower than the base species but armored enough to charge through attacks without flinching. Its danger comes from Wylk-charged forelimb swings that burst into wide explosions, plus corrosive drool that lowers defense. The fight rewards controlled wound destruction, which both topples it and cuts off its Wylk empowerment.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Corrosive drool makes its bites inflict Defense Down, and it can fling the drool at hunters.
  • Arm attacks that carry Guardian energy are telegraphed by its arms glowing white; these produce Wylk bursts.
  • Performed over Wylk pools, those attacks leave a Wylkrystal; hitting a crystal with most elemental attacks detonates it for damage of that element.
  • Like all Guardians it regenerates wounds, but destroying enough wounds topples it and stops it channeling Wylk into attacks; it re-empowers by absorbing Wylk from the ground.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Two-arm slam: rises on its hind legs and pauses before slamming.
  • Charging run that does not flinch from incoming attacks.
  • Bite combos that apply Defense Down.
  • Wylk-charged forelimb swings with delayed ground explosions.
  • Weak roar and minor tremor can briefly immobilize hunters.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • After a Wylk-based foreleg attack, the forelegs are a Focus Strike weak point; a successful strike makes it reel back and briefly topple, with a cooldown.
  • Break wounds repeatedly to topple it and deny the Wylk-empowered state.
  • Use elemental Slinger ammo on spawned Wylkrystals for burst damage and topples.
  • Stay mobile during the charge; it cannot be interrupted by ordinary hits.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fextralife lists Fire, Ice, Thunder and Dragon, and recommends Fire; Kiranico hitzones show 25 Fire on head and torso, 20 Ice on limbs and 30 Dragon only on wounds. There is no single dominant elemental weakness across sources.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Forelegs are the focus-strike weak point; wounds can be destroyed but regenerate. A complete breakable-part list was not found in the sources checked.

Recommended Skills & Items

Adamant Seed, Armorskin, Hardshell Powder or Iron Skin for Defense Down; Earplugs for the weak roar; Tremor Resistance; elemental Slinger ammo.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

None. Tempered and Frenzied variants exist as standard states.
