MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Doshaguma

Windward Plains · Scarlet Forest · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A territorial, bear-like Fanged Beast that fights with arm slams, thrown rocks and body checks. In the Windward Plains it travels in packs with a distinct alpha, which turns a straightforward duel into an add-management problem. At low health it enters a brutal rage: its muscles swell and old wounds open.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Pack behaviour in the Windward Plains; solitary in the Scarlet Forest.
  • When weakened it enrages, opening wounds that become a weak point — but it also becomes more aggressive, so the window is risky.
  • Its stomach is exposed when it stands on its hind legs and is a relatively soft target.
  • Turf war with Chatacabra is documented.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • 2-Arm Slam — rears up, pauses, then pounds the ground with each arm.
  • Arm Slam and Throw — slams, then throws the rock it picked up.
  • Ground Slam — digs both arms in, then rips up the ground and throws it.
  • Overhead Smash, Sweep Attack, Spin Attack (turns in place sweeping and biting).
  • Bite — pause, then a forward lunge; Weak Roar and Minor Tremor can immobilise you.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Hit the exposed stomach while it is upright, then clear out before the slam.
  • Punish the recovery after 2-Arm Slam and after the throw.
  • Lure it into rocks and vine traps; if a pack crashes your hunt, Dung Pods and Large Dung Pods are the answer.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fire (Monster Hunter Wiki). Fextralife also lists Thunder as effective; the monster itself has no element. Sources conflict on the secondary element.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Both forelegs and the tail (tail hair).

Recommended Skills & Items

Dung Pods / Large Dung Pods for packs, Earplugs for the weak roar, Tremor Resistance; Fire element weapons.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Guardian Doshaguma exists as a separate Ruins of Wyveria variant in the base game; no Title Update changes to the standard Doshaguma.
