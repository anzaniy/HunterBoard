MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Jin Dahaad

Iceshard Cliffs

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

Jin Dahaad is a multi-area siege fight against a huge ice leviathan rather than a single-arena duel. It moves between areas at health thresholds and mixes physical sweeps with ice breath, frostblight and a charge-up nova that is a guaranteed cart if it connects. The fight is structured around breaking its four iceplates and using arena cover and collapsible terrain.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Four iceplates (Cranial, Abdominal, Tail, Large) can be broken; breaking all of them neutralizes the freezing air on its limbs and tail.
  • The Large Iceplate is frozen over and nearly immune until the other three are broken; breaking it also disables its super attack.
  • Breaking a leg always knocks it down, giving access to the plates.
  • Whoever breaks an iceplate draws its aggro, shown by a red line like the Luring Pod effect.
  • It cannot be captured.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Frost Nova: covers itself in ice walls then unleashes an arena-sized explosion; hide behind a knocked-down ice chunk or fast travel to camp.
  • Cold Vortex: area-wide freeze that roots the hunter; mash the recovery button.
  • Ice Breath: vertical version tilts its head back, horizontal version lowers and shakes its head; charged versions are preceded by a high-pitched scream.
  • Cold Exhaust: looks down at its chest and hisses before releasing cold air that builds Frostblight rapidly.
  • Charge and Belly Flop cover long distance with continuous hitboxes.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Stay between its front and back legs; the legs are weak points and most attacks miss there.
  • Break the three outer iceplates first, then the Large plate, ideally while it is downed from a leg break.
  • In the second area, bring down the Giant Frozen Pillar with the interact button after it climbs and uses its ice breath (the pillar begins collapsing after the third), for massive damage and a guaranteed topple.
  • In the third area use collapsible walls and floating rubble; collapse the structure it clings to with the hook Slinger.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fire; resists Ice. Inflicts Iceblight and Frostblight.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

The four iceplates (Cranial, Abdominal, Tail, Large); legs break and cause knockdowns.

Recommended Skills & Items

Cleanser or Bind Resistance for Frostblight/freeze, high Ice Resistance and Defense, Fire weapons, and cover awareness.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Arch-Tempered Jin Dahaad arrived 2025-12-23 (ET) with Title Update 4 via the permanent HR 100 event quest "Heart of Judecca", later also in the 10-star quest "Frozen Knells of the East" (February 2026). Arch-Tempered changes: more aggressive combos including double body slams, final-area attacks used in the third area, ice-mist whirlwind causing rapid Frostblight, extra delayed icicle explosions, new attacks, icicle clusters while coiled, and a faster nova that is a guaranteed faint.
