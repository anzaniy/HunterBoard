MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Guardian Arkveld

Ruins of Wyveria

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A Construct built from Arkveld by the ancient civilization of Wyveria, fought as the Low Rank story climax. It fights with four retractable chainblades mounted on its wings, absorbing energy through them and discharging it as Dragon element. The arena is littered with Wylkrystals, which its attacks can detonate.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Chainblades extend, slam, and retract; retracting leaves red lightning marks that explode a moment later.
  • The chainblades themselves take no elemental damage at all — raw only on those parts.
  • Its attacks inflict Dragonblight, which temporarily negates your weapon's element/ailment. Carry Nulberries; Dragon Resistance or Blight Resistance helps.
  • Wylkrystals explode when its elemental attacks touch them, and Slinger ammo can set them off deliberately.
  • Flash Pods are effective and cancel its current action, though resistance builds with repeated use. Shock Traps also work.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Red Lightning Blasts — stands on hind legs, body glows white then red, crosses all four chainblades, then a large forward AoE that can blow hunters away.
  • Chainblade Chain Attack — right, right, then left slam, with a deliberate delay between the two right-hand hits; usually followed by Red Lightning Blasts.
  • Chainblade Somersault — body glows white, then red, before it launches backward and somersaults forward.
  • Tail Thrust — almost no windup and aimed directly behind it; dangerous while you are attacking the tail.
  • Grab — used on a hunter who is already knocked down; mash to escape.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • After Double Chainblade Attack the blades stay planted for roughly 1.5 seconds; after Tail Strike the tail is stuck for about 2 seconds. Both are clean head/wing openings.
  • Save Flash Pods for the Red Lightning Blasts setup rather than spending them at random.
  • Head, wings/chainblades and tail are the 3-star damage parts; the torso and legs are noticeably worse.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Dragon is listed as its elemental weakness, but Fextralife's elemental table shows only 1-star effectiveness on every part except the chainblades, which are immune — so element contributes little and raw is the practical choice. Poison, sleep, paralysis, blast and stun are all 2-star; Exhaust has no effect. Note the conflict: community write-ups claim Dragon is meaningfully better, the hitzone table does not support that.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, wings/chainblades and tail take the best damage and are the natural Focus Strike targets; the sources checked do not publish a separate break-part table for this monster.

Recommended Skills & Items

Flash Pods, Shock Trap, Nulberry, Dragon Resistance 3 / Blight Resistance 3, the Wylk Burst armor skill (stamina and red gauge recovery near Wylkrystals), Defender Meal. Raw weapons over element.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Appears in the Low Rank assignments "Repel the Guardian Arkveld" and "The Pale Dream of a Lone Shadow". No high-rank or Tempered version is listed in the sources checked.
