MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Blangonga

Iceshard Cliffs · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A compact, extremely mobile Fanged Beast that fights alongside a pack of Blango and uses snow and ice breath to control space. It closes distance fast, so long-range weapons are not automatically safe. Individually it is not a damage wall; the difficulty is the pack plus its freeze setup.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Commands its Blango pack; one Blango is ordered to attack on its behalf.
  • Breaking both fangs (head broken twice) permanently stops it issuing commands.
  • Frost breath freezes hunters in place; snowballs are thrown at range.
  • In its area 14 nest it burrows into deep snow and re-emerges under the hunter.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Swirling Smash: roar, punches around, then a 360° swipe with heavy knockback.
  • Forward Leap: telegraphed by a roar; long recovery, easy to sidestep.
  • Frost Breath: cone that immobilises on hit (Cleanser/Nulberry needed).
  • Weak Roar (Earplugs), plus the burrow ambush in the nest.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • During frost breath its rear is exposed; a Focus Strike there makes it reel and topple (has a cooldown).
  • Clear the Blango first, then fight it alone; break fangs to stop pack commands.
  • Head and tail are the weakest zones; rear is a listed weak point in some guides.
  • Pitfall/Shock Traps and Flash Pods work; Luring Pods can start a turf war.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fire and Thunder. Resists Ice and Dragon; Sonic Bombs have no effect. Status effects are broadly effective (sources differ slightly on which are best).

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Fangs (head) twice; tail breakable but not severable.

Recommended Skills & Items

Earplugs, Cleanser/Nulberry, Hot Drink, Flash Pods, traps.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Base-game High Rank monster. TU4 added 9★ Tempered versions of Blangonga (with Ajarakan and Guardian Fulgur Anjanath) as rotating event quests. No Arch-Tempered form.
