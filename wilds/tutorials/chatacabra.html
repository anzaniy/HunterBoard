Chatacabra

Windward Plains · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

An early Low Rank amphibian and the target of the quest "Beware the Chatacabra". It knuckle-walks on heavy forelimbs, licks up ore and stone when agitated, and fights with a very long sticky tongue plus wide, heavy arm swings. Its hitzones are generous, so the fight is mostly about reading slow, telegraphed commits rather than surviving pressure.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Sticky mucus stored in the forelimb scutes lets it glue rocks and ore to its arms, widening the reach and power of its strikes.
  • The tongue has long reach and is used both to grab prey and to attack; it is also a soft, high-damage target when extended.
  • Turf war with Doshaguma is documented.
  • It can be baited into swallowing Barrel Bombs whole (Hunter's Notes / ecology).
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Overhead Smash — backs up and raises both forelegs before slamming down.
  • Rushing charge — recoils, pauses, then charges with mouth wide open.
  • Spinning attack — spins in place while dragging its tongue along the ground.
  • Diving attack — telegraphed by a full-body shake before it leaps and lands on top of you.
  • Stomp and a deafening roar.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Hit the exposed tongue and the ore-packed forelegs; both are among its best hitzones.
  • Lure a charge into the wall fissures in the arena — it gets stuck and gives a long free damage window.
  • Flash Pods, Shock Traps and Pitfall Traps are all listed as effective; it has a unique pitfall animation where it flips onto its back.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Thunder (Monster Hunter Wiki). Fextralife additionally lists Ice as a weakness and Dragon as a resistance — sources conflict on Ice.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Both forelegs (break rewards are guaranteed on left and right foreleg).

Recommended Skills & Items

Flash Pod, Shock Trap, Pitfall Trap; Ice Attack or Thunder Attack plus Critical Element / Charge Master (Fextralife).
