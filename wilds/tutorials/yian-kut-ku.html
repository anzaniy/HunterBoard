MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Yian Kut-Ku

Scarlet Forest · Iceshard Cliffs · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

The High Rank entry monster, first fought in the 4-star assignment "Lured by Abundance". A fast, jumpy Bird Wyvern that spits fireballs and pecks in long chains. It frequently appears in pairs or clusters of up to four, which is the real difficulty of the fight rather than its individual moves.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Oversized, sensitive ears: Sonic/Screamer Pods stun it in place and give a free combo window — but it enrages immediately afterwards and may become immune while angry.
  • Herd behaviour: bring Dung Pods to split the group and fight one at a time.
  • Its fire attacks inflict Fireblight; roll three times, use a Nulberry, or roll through water.
  • Capturable: weaken it, then Pitfall or Shock Trap plus two Tranq Bombs.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Side-to-side fireball spit — charges forward spitting left and right; much faster when enraged.
  • Lobbed fireball — rears up first, so the tell is obvious; dodge sideways.
  • Charge — hops into position, digs its beak into the ground, then charges.
  • Peck — several pecks in a row that can stunlock you; the single biggest cart risk.
  • Tail swipe and low rush — low damage but constant disruption.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Dodge pecks sideways or through its legs and attack from the flank; do not stand in front.
  • Wings are the best sever hitzone (16), head the best blunt and shot hitzone, neck the best Ice hitzone.
  • Use the Sonic Pod stun for your longest combo, then disengage before the enrage.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Ice is the best element (6 on the head, 8 on the neck); Thunder is a solid second (4 head, 6 neck). Dragon is 0 on the head, torso and wings. Fextralife lists Thunder/Ice as the weaknesses and Dragon as resisted; IGN also lists Water — treat Ice/Thunder as the safe picks.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head only (breaking it guarantees a Kut-Ku Ear).

Recommended Skills & Items

Screamer Pods, Dung Pods, Nulberry, Pitfall/Shock Trap and Tranq Bombs, standard healing.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES
