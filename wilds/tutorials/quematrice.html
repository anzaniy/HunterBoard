MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Quematrice

Windward Plains · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A small, stocky Brute Wyvern that scavenges carrion rather than hunting, and is first hunted in the ★2 Assignment "Hot on Their Tails". Its threat comes from a long, muscular tail that lays down flammable oil and then ignites it, plus wide sweeping body turns. The flame itself burns briefly, so the real danger is the tail's reach and knockback.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • The tail secretes an oily fluid ("Igniter") that vaporises quickly; swinging the tail releases it as a grey gas that settles on the ground.
  • Sparks from rubbing the tail's pleats together or against the ground ignite the gas; more viscous fluid burns hotter.
  • Damaging or severing the tail makes spark generation harder, which is the single biggest way to defuse the fight.
  • Oil released in water cannot be ignited.
  • Researchers in-game suggest the blaze is mainly a threat display, not a primary weapon.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Pecking attack, up to four pecks in a row.
  • Beak sweep after a short pause.
  • Tail Slam and Tail Sweep — both blow hunters away; the sweep can also launch Palicoes.
  • Fireblight Tail Sweep — same sweep but ignites the ground along the tail's arc.
  • Rush attack (backs off, pauses, charges mouth open) and Spin and Sweep (tucks tail, spins, then sweeps upward).
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Watch where the oil settles to predict where fire will spread, then fight outside those lanes.
  • Cut the tail early; head and tail are its best physical hitzones.
  • Punish the recovery after Spin and Sweep and after the rush, which commits it forward.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Water; resists Fire. Inflicts Fireblight.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head and tail; the tail is severable (tail carve rewards).

Recommended Skills & Items

Water element weapons; Nulberry, Fire Resistance or Blight Resistance for Fireblight; Flash Pod and both trap types are listed effective.
