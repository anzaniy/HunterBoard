MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Nu Udra

Oilwell Basin · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

Nu Udra is a six-armed cephalopod boss of the Oilwell Basin that alternates between physical tentacle pressure and a flaming enraged state. Each arm can be severed individually, which permanently shortens its reach and adds carves. Fighting it head-on keeps its tells readable, and its own enrage can be punished by forcing it out of the state.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Six grounded arms attack in nearly any direction, often several at once; each arm can be severed to reduce range and give an extra carve.
  • When enraged it ignites its oilsilt coating, adding fire damage to tentacle moves, flaming oil projectiles with delayed explosions, and a fire siphon.
  • It eventually sheds the oil coating in a rain of flaming oil and then reapplies it.
  • Enough damage during the enrage knocks it out of the state, strips the coating and topples it.
  • It is blind and therefore immune to Flash Bombs; in its Area 17 nest it burrows through ground cracks to reposition.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Multi Slam: rears up and slams down; in High Rank it can chain two slams with variable delay.
  • Flame Breath: sustained fire stream; blockable but drains stamina heavily.
  • Grab and Burn: grabs the hunter, pauses, then breathes fire on them; mash the knife or hit the weak point with a Slinger pod.
  • Magma Eruption: erupts and rains magma around itself, ending its ignited state.
  • Octo Leap and fireballs cover long distance and punish passive spacing.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • After the rear-up body slam its beak, behind the face tentacles, is exposed; a Focus Strike topples it, with a cooldown.
  • Focus the arms for reachable damage and sever them for extra carves; switch to head/mouth once it is down.
  • Douse burning tentacles with Puddle Pods to weaken its attacks.
  • Fight from the front rather than flanking; roll three times to clear Fireblight without sheathing.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Water; resists Fire. Inflicts Fireblight.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

All six arms can be severed individually.

Recommended Skills & Items

Puddle Pods, Nulberry, Fire Resistance, traps; note Flash Pods do nothing.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Arch-Tempered Nu Udra arrived 2025-10-21 (ET) during Title Update 3 via the permanent HR 100 event quest "Be Cursed, Ye Shadowy Flame", and was later added to the 10-star quest "Scion of the Dark Cradle" in the February 2026 update. Arch-Tempered changes: faster, longer-reaching moves; a burning aura while ignited that is negated by Adaptability Lv2; it no longer leaves the ignited state unless forced out; new attacks.
