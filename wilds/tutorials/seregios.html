MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Seregios

Windward Plains · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A hyper-aggressive Flying Wyvern that fights at speed and at range, flinging blade-like scales that cause Bleeding. It spends long stretches airborne, so the hunt is a hit-and-run rhythm rather than a stand-and-trade.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Bleeding: triggered by scale hits and talon strikes; you lose health whenever you roll or move. Crouch and wait, or eat a Ration/Well-done Steak to clear it.
  • Scales that land stay on the ground and can still cut you; some can be picked up as Bladescale Pods.
  • It grabs small herbivores and throws them at hunters.
  • Part breaks reduce scale output — breaking head/wings cuts volleys, tail sever removes rear-facing ones (community estimate, not an official number).
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Ultimate dive from high circling — dodge sideways twice.
  • Sweeping talon attack that leaves a leg extended.
  • Scale volleys followed immediately by a follow-up attack.
  • High-flight grab/pin.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • After the talon sweep or a divebomb, one leg is exposed — Focus Strike there topples it (cooldown).
  • Flash Pods knock it out of the air; otherwise wait for landing recovery.
  • Legs soften when enraged; head is the usual weak spot.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Thunder primary; Ice secondary. Fire is a null (no effect); Dragon and Water resisted. Poison, Sleep, Paralysis, Blast, Stun and Exhaust all land; Sonic Bombs do nothing.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, both wings, both hind legs, tail (severable).

Recommended Skills & Items

Rations for Bleed, Divine Blessing, Flash Pods, Weakness Exploit, Evade Extender.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Title Update 2 (30 Jun 2025), HR 31 unlock; Tempered HR 41+; 9★ at HR 100. No Arch-Tempered form.
