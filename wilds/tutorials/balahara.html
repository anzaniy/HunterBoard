MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Balahara

Windward Plains · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A sand-swimming Leviathan that builds sand pits and ambushes anything crossing them. It fights with a drill-like tail, dark-green mucus and water spit, and it can reposition underground between areas. Much of the fight is terrain management: the pits it leaves behind are hazards for you and for other monsters.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Sand pits can swallow other large monsters; you can still follow a monster that gets pulled in.
  • It relies on heightened hearing while submerged, so Sonic Bombs and Screamer Pods are highly effective at forcing it out.
  • The inside of its mouth is soft and can be exploited right after it expels mucus.
  • Inflicts Waterblight (slower stamina recovery).
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Spit Attack — up to three in a row, applies Waterblight and blows hunters away on a direct hit.
  • Tail Thrust, Tail Sweep (tucks tail first) and Tail Charge (clutches tail, then spins forward).
  • Face Slam — brief charge with mouth open, then slams its face into the ground.
  • Charged Swipe — backs away, raises a foreleg, then swipes.
  • Dive Attack — climbs to high ground, spits, then dives mouth-first.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • After a spit sequence, hit the open mouth.
  • Use Screamer Pods/Sonic Bombs while it is burrowed to bring it up on your terms.
  • Do not fight standing in its sand pits; reposition to solid ground before committing to long combos.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Thunder; resists Water.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head and tail.

Recommended Skills & Items

Screamer Pod / Sonic Bomb (Baunos), Nulberry or Water Resistance for Waterblight, Thunder element weapons.
