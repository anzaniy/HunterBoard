MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Uth Duna

Scarlet Forest

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

The water-veiled apex Leviathan of the Scarlet Forest, first met as a repel in the ★2 Assignment "A Feast in the Deep" and first hunted properly in the ★6 Assignment "Tide Overtaking Timber". It fights by throwing its considerable weight around to generate waves, and its gelatinous veil both armours it and adds mass to every slam.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • The veil reduces the damage you deal; stripping it is the first objective of the fight.
  • The veil falls away once it loses enough water — at which point it becomes faster and more aggressive.
  • Underwater Dive can replenish the veil/protective skin, so the armour can come back.
  • Inflicts Waterblight (slower stamina recovery).
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Twisted Body Slam — twists like a tornado, launches forward, slams with a wide AoE and a water wave; can repeat twice.
  • Underwater Dive — same wave effect but from below; also restores its covering.
  • Body Slam, Belly Flop, Dive Combo, Arm Sweep (blows hunters away), Overhead Smash / Overhead Sweep, double Bite.
  • Weak Roar.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Dodge the wave on timing, or use Wedge Beetles to get above it.
  • Head and forelegs are weak to cut and blunt; the head takes more blunt damage, making Hammer KOs a strong plan.
  • Punish after Twisted Body Slam and after the dive recovery, when it is stationary.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Thunder; resists Water.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, both forelegs, tail.

Recommended Skills & Items

Nulberry, Water Resistance or Blight Resistance; Earplugs; Thunder element weapons.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Arch-Tempered Uth Duna arrived with Title Update 2 and became permanently available on 22 July 2025 (8★ Event Quest "These Roots Run Deep", HR50+), unlocking Uth Duna γ armour. It gained new moves (Barrel Roll, Wide Stomp, Lunging Bite). Ver. 1.030.02 (23 Oct 2025) fixed 9★ Arch-Tempered Uth Duna hide toughness being higher than intended, and Ver. 1.041 (Feb 2026) added 10★ Arch-Tempered hunts.
