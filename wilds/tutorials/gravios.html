MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Gravios

Oilwell Basin · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · MEDIUM confidence

A heavily armoured Flying Wyvern whose hide deflects most attacks. The fight is a damage-uptime puzzle rather than a dodge test: you have to find the parts you can actually hurt and the weapon setups that ignore its armour. Quests include the 5-star "Armor Like a Mountain", the 5-star "Succulent Scraps" and the 6-star "Troublemakers".

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • The armoured shell deflects attacks below a sharpness threshold — bring blue sharpness or better, or use Mind's Eye, shelling, or phial damage to bypass it.
  • The head deflects; do not treat it as a weak point from the front.
  • Gas attacks: purple gas inflicts Poison, blue gas inflicts Sleep.
  • Fire element does nothing to it — 0 across every hitzone.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • The beam — a long, straight, high-damage laser with a clear charge-up; this is the attack that punishes greedy combos.
  • Poison and sleep gas clouds that linger and set up the beam or a charge.
  • Rolling/charging body slam and stomps at close range.
  • Tail sweeps when you are behind it.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • The belly is the standout weak point (Water 7) and opens up when it topples or after the beam.
  • Break the belly and back to keep the damage flowing, then work the legs to trip it.
  • Stay to the side during the beam windup and move in as it finishes.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Water is the best element, best applied to the belly. Fire is 0 everywhere.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, belly, back, wings, legs, and tail (severable).

Recommended Skills & Items

Water weapons, Mind's Eye or high sharpness, Poison/Sleep resistance or Nulberries, Antidote.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

A 6-star Tempered version exists; no Frenzied variant listed.
