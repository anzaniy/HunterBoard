MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Rompopolo

Oilwell Basin · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A bizarre, indigo Brute Wyvern covered in inflatable gas sacs, fought in the Oilwell Basin's oilsilt. It mixes poison clouds with explosive injections into the oil, so the arena itself becomes part of the threat. The sacs are also the answer: swelling sacs can be struck to deflate them.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Anterior sacs hold poison; posterior sacs are explosive.
  • A swelling sac can be struck to shrink it back down, cancelling the coming attack.
  • It injects gas into oilsilt with its stinger, making the ground bubble and then explode.
  • Blasts in oilsilt kick up rubble that becomes stable footing; on normal ground the tail strike throws up stony rubble.
  • Inflicts Poison.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Poison Cloud — inflated head pulls back, then swings left to right venting poison.
  • Poisonous Charge — rises, steps forward twice while venting smoke.
  • Tail Strike — raises tail, slams; the ground around the tail bubbles and then explodes. Deflates the tail.
  • Tail Thrust — inflated tail drives into the oil, then the body collapses onto it, causing blasts around it and blowing hunters away.
  • Tail Whip, Tail Whip and Strike, Tail Whip and Sweep; Double Arm Overhead Strike; Ram when you camp the head; opening roar.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Pop swollen sacs on sight — it converts its own tells into free damage.
  • After Tail Thrust it collapses onto its tail, giving a clear window.
  • Fight out of the oilsilt lanes so the explosions are not under your feet.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Water; resists Poison.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, back, both forelegs, tail.

Recommended Skills & Items

Antidote / Herbal Medicine / Herbal Powder, Poison Resistance; Water element weapons; Earplugs for the weak roar.
