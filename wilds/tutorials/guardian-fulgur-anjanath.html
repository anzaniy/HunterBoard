Guardian Fulgur Anjanath

Ruins of Wyveria

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · MEDIUM confidence

A Construct version of Fulgur Anjanath, rebuilt by the Wyveria civilization and charged with lightning. It is a bruising, charge-heavy fight in the Ruins, with Guardian crystals scattered around the arena that can be used against it.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Thunder attacks build Thunderblight on hunters.
  • Its back wounds are the source of its electrical discharge — destroying them temporarily disables the lightning.
  • Guardian crystals in the arena can be detonated with Puddle, Frost or Dragon Pods.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Charging bite/ram that covers a long distance and can chain into a second pass.
  • Body slam and tail swings at close range.
  • Lightning discharge from the back when it is enraged.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Focus the back wounds to switch off the lightning, then move to the head and legs.
  • Use crystal detonations as free damage and stagger opportunities.
  • Fight from the flank; standing in front of a charging Anjanath is how the quest ends early.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Ice is the best element, with values of 7 on the nose and wings.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, legs and tail (tail is severable).

Recommended Skills & Items

Ice weapons, Thunder Resistance or Thunderblight cures, Puddle/Frost/Dragon Pods for the crystals.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Quests include the 5-star "Thundering Flowers", the 5-star "Guardian Fulgur Anjanath Hunt" and the event quest "Running Wild". A 6-star Tempered version appears after "Storm-cold Vortex".
