MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Rathalos

Scarlet Forest · Oilwell Basin · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · MEDIUM confidence

The male Flying Wyvern and the series' benchmark aerial fight, unlocked in High Rank Chapter 4 after "New Ecosystems" and featured in the 5-star optional "King of the Skies". He spends most of the fight airborne, so the entire matchup is about getting him back on the ground.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Breaking the wings measurably reduces his air time — this is the priority break.
  • Flash Pods knock him out of the sky.
  • A Focus Strike on the mouth after his fireball is a reliable stagger.
  • Opening wounds while he is airborne can ground him.
  • Rathian may enter the locale — use Dung Pods to separate them.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Aerial claw dive — he climbs, hovers, then drops; the hover is the tell.
  • Fireball volleys, including a three-shot spread while hovering.
  • Tail spin and the poison-bearing aerial tail attack.
  • Roar into a combo, which is the usual cart setup.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • The moment he lands is your damage window; hit the head, not the wings, once they are broken.
  • Break wings first, then commit to head damage.
  • Keep Flash Pods for the moment he starts hovering rather than wasting them on the ground.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Dragon is best, but values are low (maximum 6), so raw is competitive.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, wings and tail (tail is severable).

Recommended Skills & Items

Flash Pods, Dung Pods, Earplugs or a shield against the roar combo, Poison resistance if Rathian is present.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

A 6-star Tempered version exists; no Frenzied variant listed.
