MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Omega Planetes

Iceshard Cliffs

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

The FFXIV crossover superboss — a four-legged machine built like an MMO raid boss. Fights are won by doing mechanics, not by DPS uptime: the fight telegraphs major moves in the message log, expects a tank to hold enmity, and punishes a failed mechanic with a team wipe.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Message-bar warnings announce the next big attack (e.g. Wave Cannon).
  • Pantokrator Mode: enraged phase with rapid-fire laser patterns.
  • Delta Attack: it spawns a Nerscylla clone; kill it fast (Fire is strong on Nerscylla) to generate the shield that blocks the attack. Failure means a wipe — if the hunt survives, Omega becomes permanently more damaging and tankier.
  • Shields also come from destroying glowing blue Omega Micros, or from Shield Generator ammo off broken legs.
  • Immune to traps, Poison, Sleep, Exhaust and Flash.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Starboard/Larboard Wave Cannon — covers one side; the log warns you.
  • Frontal beams and large laser; head becomes a weak point during/after them.
  • Rocket punch (offset-attack and power-clashable), body slam, flamethrower combo (legs exposed afterwards).
  • Missile rain during phase transitions.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Focus Strike the head after beam attacks, and the legs after the flamethrower combo.
  • Bring a Thunder weapon plus a Fire secondary for the Nerscylla clone; swap via Seikret.
  • Use Cactuars/Morbols/Mog of the Ages to stagger the clone; Pictomancer item is a large free damage source.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Thunder and Dragon; Fire is a null. Blast is the effective status.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, back, legs/rear power unit (leg breaks feed shield ammo).

Recommended Skills & Items

Fire Resistance, group healing/Wide-Range, Thunder weapon + Fire backup, Pictomancer.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Title Update 3 (29 Sep 2025), HR 41 collab unlock; 9★ permanent quest at HR 100. "Savage" (Planetes Protocol) is the hard variant: phase-3 moveset from the start, freezing mist (Frostblight), and a second Wave Cannon covering the previously safe side. No Arch-Tempered form.
