MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Rathian

Windward Plains · Scarlet Forest · Oilwell Basin · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · MEDIUM confidence

The classic female Flying Wyvern, unlocked in High Rank Chapter 4 after "New Ecosystems". She is a ground-oriented fight built around poison, fireballs and the backflip, and is far more readable than Rathalos. Quests include the 4-star optional "All Hail the Queen", the event quest "Like a Fire Hidden by Sand", the 5-star "Continuing Conflict" and a 5-star Arena quest.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Poison comes from the tail — the backflip and tail flip are the main sources. Severing the tail removes most of the poison threat.
  • Flash Pods work and are the standard answer to her hovering.
  • A Focus Strike on the mouth during her fireball animation is a reliable stagger.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Backflip — she turns her back to you and lifts off; the tail sweep comes over her head. This is the signature poison hit.
  • Triple fireball — three aimed fireballs in sequence; the pause afterwards is the punish.
  • Running charge and tail spin at close range.
  • Hovering fireball strafes.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Attack the legs and wings while she is grounded; the head is the damage target when she lands after a backflip.
  • Cut the tail early to defuse the poison, then focus wings for the break.
  • Flash her out of the air instead of waiting.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Dragon is technically the best element, but every value is very low (maximum 6), so raw generally outperforms element.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Wings and tail (tail is severable).

Recommended Skills & Items

Flash Pods, Poison Resistance or Antidote/Herbal Medicine, Dung Pods if Rathalos joins.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

A 5-star Tempered version exists; no Frenzied variant listed.
