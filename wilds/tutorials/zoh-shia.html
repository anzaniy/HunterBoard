MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Zoh Shia

Ruins of Wyveria

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · MEDIUM confidence

The final boss of Low Rank and a slay-only encounter — it cannot be captured. The fight runs through three phases: a crystallized shell phase, a fire phase, and a "Fallen State" phase where it attacks with red lightning. Its body is partly made of Wylk crystal, which is both its armour and its weakness.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Wylkcrystals in the arena can be detonated with elemental Slinger ammo, damaging the monster.
  • Every third wound you open triggers a knockdown.
  • A Focus Strike on the exposed wingarms knocks it down, but this has a cooldown rather than working every time.
  • It is immune to traps and to Flash Pods — bring neither as a solution.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Fire phase attacks that leave burning ground and wide flame sweeps.
  • Fallen State red lightning attacks with very large arcs and delayed secondary blasts.
  • Wingarm slams and body checks that cover most of the arena floor.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • The wingarms are by far the best raw hitzones (cut/blunt in the 80-85 range); the head is the stun target (100 blunt).
  • Bank wounds deliberately so the third one lands when you have a big opening, and use the Focus Strike knockdown on the wingarms.
  • Elemental damage is a minor contributor here; raw and part damage do the work.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Elemental values are low across the board, with Dragon the highest at roughly 30. Status thresholds are published (poison 150 rising to 550, etc.), so status is viable but not fast.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Wingarms and head; wound generation is the core damage loop.

Recommended Skills & Items

Elemental Slinger ammo (Fire/Water/Thunder/Ice/Dragon Pods) for the crystals, Partbreaker to accelerate wounds, healing and defense items. Do not rely on traps or Flashes.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Rematched in the High Rank 8-star quest "Germination" and the High Rank 10-star event quest "Hope of the Ancients".
