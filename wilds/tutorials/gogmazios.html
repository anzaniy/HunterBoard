MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Gogmazios

Oilwell Basin

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A siege-scale Elder Dragon encased in black oil, fought across three phases in three areas with heavy environmental tools. Up to 8 hunters participate (4 players + 4 Support Hunters). Its elemental matchup changes with its oil state, which is the core of the fight.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Three oil states: Solid (hard hide, Fire softens it), Liquid (soft hide, Dragon works better, tar drips), Gaseous (final area; state changes stop, Dragon is extremely effective, oil explodes where it lands).
  • Breaking the back dislodges the Convergence Dragonator; charge it at the wall coupling to 100% before firing for massive damage.
  • Smaller dragonators and siege emplacements are scattered through the arena and are a major damage source.
  • Its unavoidable-looking blast can be weakened with Dragon damage, blocked with a shield, or avoided on high ground (high ground is destroyed after first use).
  • Mutated Armaments on its body only take damage in Liquid/Gaseous states and yield extra Tarred Devices.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Area heat ray that raises lava from the floor — move to the northern high ground; the floor burns.
  • Sweeping tar beams and tar projectiles; tar puddles detonate in the gaseous phase.
  • Flamethrower sweeps while mobile; heavy wing-arm slams.
  • Roar is strong (Earplugs), tremor is minor.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Topple it by focusing the wing-legs, then break the back during the knockdown.
  • Plan the nearest siege weapon, not the nearest body part, after each knockdown.
  • Immune to all traps and Flash Pods, so control comes from the arena.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Dragon is the true weakness; Fire is required to soften solid oil. Water, Thunder and Ice are null. Blast and Stun are effective; Poison, Sleep and Paralysis are not.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, chest, back, both wings, tail; Mutated Armaments (liquid/gas only).

Recommended Skills & Items

Fire Resistance 20 (nullifies Fireblight), Tremor Resistance, an oil/sludge movement skill, Cool Drink for the lava phase, Fire + Dragon weapons.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Title Update 4, 16 Dec 2025 (some outlets list 15 Dec by timezone). Introduced Gogma Artian weapon upgrades and the armour limit-break system. No Arch-Tempered form; cannot be Tempered.
