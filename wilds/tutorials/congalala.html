MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Congalala

Scarlet Forest · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A pink-furred Fanged Beast whose whole kit revolves around stench, poison and blast gas. It grazes on mushrooms and can pick up rocks to throw, and its breath attacks change with what it has eaten. The fight is less about damage racing and more about not being locked out of healing.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Stench prevents the use of health and stamina recovery items; cleared by Deodorant or by evading in water.
  • It digs up mushrooms and eats them to enable breath attacks; it can also grab a boulder and throw it.
  • Inflicts Poison, Paralysis, Blastblight and Minor Tremor in addition to stench.
  • Blastblight detonates after a delay or on a knockback hit.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Stench Bomb and Breath Bomb — odour clouds that apply the no-healing debuff.
  • Tail Snatch — picks up a mushroom or rock; the held item telegraphs breath or a thrown boulder.
  • Overhead Smash (backs away, raises a foreleg), Body Slam (rears onto hind legs, pauses, slams).
  • Fury Swipes — up to four alternating arm swings; it topples onto its back at the end.
  • Dive Attack and Dive and Swipe — crouches low, then leaps and lands on its belly or swipes on landing.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Fury Swipes ends with it on its back — the clearest punish in the fight.
  • Interrupt or punish the mushroom-eating animation before the breath comes out.
  • Keep Deodorant ready so a stench application does not cost you a cart.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fire (Monster Hunter Wiki). Fextralife also lists Ice as effective — sources conflict on the secondary element.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, both forelegs, tail.

Recommended Skills & Items

Deodorant, Stench Resistance, Poison Resistance, Blast Resistance, Tremor Resistance; Flash Pod and both trap types listed effective.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

None in Wilds (no Wilds-specific variant; Emerald Congalala is a separate older monster).
