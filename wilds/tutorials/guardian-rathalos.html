Guardian Rathalos

Ruins of Wyveria

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

Guardian Rathalos is an artificial Rathalos that leans harder on ground talon attacks than the original while keeping the classic fire breath and poison talons. Its talon moves seed Wylk bursts and crystals that its own fire attacks can detonate, so the arena becomes part of the threat. Breaking its wounds both topples it and shuts down its Wylk channeling.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Poisonous talons and flaming breath are its core tools, with more ground-based talon work than base Rathalos.
  • Talon attacks that carry Guardian energy are telegraphed by glowing white legs and create Wylk bursts.
  • Over Wylk pools those attacks leave Wylkrystals; elemental attacks detonate them, and Guardian Rathalos' own fire attacks can also set them off.
  • Guardians regenerate wounds, but enough wound destruction topples it and stops Wylk channeling; it re-empowers by absorbing Wylk.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Stomp and Fireball: leaps with glowing talons, stomps for a small explosion, then turns and spits a fireball.
  • Talon Drop and Dragon Stomp: fast descending talon strikes with explosions at the feet.
  • Dragon Thrashing: hovering exploding talon strikes followed by a stomp.
  • Fire Sweep: flies toward the hunter breathing fire while spinning; needs a dive-evade to escape.
  • Flame Bath: after changing areas it channels a white aura and develops blue markings, apparently enhancing its damage.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • After any fire breath its mouth is a weak point; after a divebomb its legs are a weak point. Focus Strikes on either make it reel back and topple, but both share one cooldown.
  • Bring Flash Pods to ground it while it flies.
  • Break the wings to reduce flight capability and sever the tail to weaken tail attacks.
  • Stay clear of Wylkrystals when it uses fire, and detonate them with Thunder or Dragon pods when it is near.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Thunder and Dragon; resists Fire. Inflicts Fireblight and Poison.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Wings and tail (severable) are the priority; the head is also a standard break target.

Recommended Skills & Items

Nulberry, Flash Pods, Thunder/Dragon Slinger pods, traps.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

None. Tempered and Frenzied variants exist as standard states.
