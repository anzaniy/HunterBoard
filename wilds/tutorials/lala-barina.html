MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Lala Barina

Scarlet Forest · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A rose-covered Temnoceran that moves in pirouettes and fights almost entirely around paralysis. It sheds petal-like florets that drift down and paralyse on repeated contact, and it can hang from the ceiling in its nest. Individual florets are weak, so the fight rewards clean spacing and punishing its long, balletic commitments.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Red florets on the abdomen carry a neuroplegic agent; when agitated it opens them like a flower and releases them.
  • Petal Rain shakes off every floret on its body — after this it is effectively disarmed until they regrow.
  • Pirouette Spin & Rain sheds only some petals in a smaller area.
  • The abdominal stinger makes attacking from behind risky, but the stinger stays exposed and vulnerable when the monster is tired.
  • In ceiling/nest terrain, sustained attacks knock it down.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Petal Rain and Mini Petal Rain — slow-falling petals; repeated hits paralyse.
  • Pirouette Spin (up to two spins, pause, two more) and Pirouette Spin & Rain.
  • Stinger Thrust, up to twice in a row, aimed behind it.
  • Bite (can paralyse), Jab with alternating arms, Leg Thrust, Hindleg Thrust, Charged Attack.
  • Tempered addition: Double Hind Legs Strike — it rears, wiggles both hind legs, slams down, then is stuck in place for several seconds.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Attack hard immediately after a full Petal Rain, when it has no florets left.
  • The Double Hind Legs Strike recovery is the largest free window in the Tempered fight.
  • Avoid loitering behind it; approach from the front/side and rotate with its spins.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fire; resists Water and Dragon. Inflicts Paralysis.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Both claws and the petals.

Recommended Skills & Items

Paralysis Resistance Lv3 (Antipara Jewel) or repeated movement/button input to shorten paralysis; Fire element weapons; Flash Pod and traps listed effective.
