MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Xu Wu

Ruins of Wyveria

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

Xu Wu is a blind cephalopod assassin that turns its mucus into blade-like limbs and hunts other monsters, including Guardians. It attacks almost entirely with its arms and creates its biggest opening by rearing up and exposing the mouth on its underside. The fight is about baiting that mouth exposure while respecting a fast, long-reaching arm game.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Uses mucus that hardens into golden blades on the limb it is about to attack with, and can throw those blades as projectiles.
  • Front arms can only be broken, while middle and back arms can be severed to reduce range.
  • It occasionally rears up and exposes its underside mouth before lunging; flinching it during the wind-up puts it in a special toppled state with the mouth exposed.
  • Hitting its mouth with Slinger ammo during a grab also triggers that exposed state.
  • It has no eyes, so it is immune to Flash Bombs; in its Area 12 nest it burrows and can emerge from the ceiling directly into a lunge.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Grab Attack: a front swipe into a grab; use any Slinger ammo to break free before it devours the hunter.
  • Dig Attack: disappears underground and drops from above with its mouth exposed.
  • Blade Slam and Leaping Combo: jumps, throws blades mid-air, then slams down.
  • Tentacle Sweep: slams a front tentacle then rotates clockwise; a dive-evade clears the range.
  • Rotating Strike and Piercing Tentacle punish standing close or directly in front.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Punish the exposed mouth after the rear-up lunge and after a flinch during the wind-up; it is the main weak point.
  • Keep Slinger ammo ready specifically to escape the grab.
  • Use Shock/Pitfall Traps, Great Thunderbugs to paralyze, Mounting, Ghillie Mantle sneak attacks on area changes, and Lump of Wyrmsbane for burst.
  • Break the mouth, head and arms, and sever the four hind tentacles for extra rewards.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Ice; resists Dragon. Sources disagree on its own element: Fextralife lists Dragon, while Game8 lists no element.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Mouth, head and arms; front arms break only, middle and back arms sever, and four hind tentacles can be severed.

Recommended Skills & Items

Slinger pods, Shock and Pitfall Traps, Ghillie Mantle, Great Thunderbugs, Lump of Wyrmsbane.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

None. Tempered Xu Wu exists as a standard endgame state.
