MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Rey Dau

Windward Plains · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

The apex Flying Wyvern of the Windward Plains, first fought in the ★2 Assignment "A Merciless Glare" and again in the ★6 Assignment "Roaring Rey Dau". It absorbs lightning strikes through its conductive shell, stores the charge, and discharges it through its horns and limbs. Its signature twist is that charged wing and tail contacts fuse ground into fulgurite glass, armouring the limb and extending its reach.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • When a charged wing or tail touches the ground it grows a fulgurite mantle: wider attack range and greater charging capacity. Breaking those parts is the counterplay.
  • Its head can be vulnerable right after a discharge attack.
  • Inflicts Thunderblight (makes you easier to stun).
  • Fights in an agitated state in High Rank due to the altered Sandtide.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Slicing Earth — plants an arm, slices forward, leaves a trail of electricity on the ground.
  • Burst of Lightning — body crackles, then a thunder burst from the mouth.
  • Charged Smash — rears for an overhead that bursts electricity on the ground; it is vulnerable during this.
  • Charged Tail Slam, Tail Slam and Sweep, Arm Sweep.
  • Diving Arm — flies up and slams an arm down, leaving a follow-up window; Thrusting Horn rush.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Break wings and tail to cut its charge capacity and shorten its reach.
  • Take the free hits during Charged Smash and after Diving Arm.
  • Bring thunder-resistant armour; Ice is the damage answer, Water the fallback.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Ice (primary, effective on all parts, best on the head); Water as secondary. Resists Thunder.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Both wings, the horns, and the tail.

Recommended Skills & Items

Thunder Resistance / Blight Resistance, Nulberry; Ice Attack (or Water Attack) with Critical Element; Earplugs for the weak roar.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Arch-Tempered Rey Dau was the first Arch-Tempered monster, added in Title Update 1 (29 April 2025) and made permanently available on 22 July 2025 via the 8★ Event Quest "A Silent Flash" (HR50+), unlocking Rey Dau γ armour and the Lord's Soul / Guts skills. Ver. 1.041 (Feb 2026) added 10★ Arch-Tempered Rey Dau.
