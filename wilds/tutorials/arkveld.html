MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Arkveld

Windward Plains · Scarlet Forest · Oilwell Basin · Iceshard Cliffs · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

The flagship "White Wraith" — a Flying Wyvern whose wings end in extendable chainblades. It absorbs elemental energy through the blades and releases it as Dragon bursts. Range is the real threat: it covers ground with leaps and lashes that reach across the arena.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Chainblades extend/retract; scraping and whipping attacks, plus long-range lashes.
  • Enraged "overloaded" state: chainblades stop being element-immune and become extremely element-weak; enough damage collapses a blade, overloading both topples it.
  • It exits the state when exhausted but enters it more often as health drops.
  • Guardian Arkveld additionally creates Wylk crystals; regular Arkveld does not.
  • Broken chainblades drop a shard that becomes a Heavy Slicing Pod.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • 180° chainblade sweep (widest hitbox — move out early).
  • Double chainblade slam down the centre (long recovery afterwards).
  • Sequential whip-pull-whip that leaves a blade extended.
  • Leaping repositions and Dragon-element bursts on landing.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Target the chainblades: they are the main weak point and break for extra materials.
  • The double slam and the extended-blade pause are the reliable damage windows.
  • Head and tail are also weak; mounting builds wounds for extra knockdowns.
  • Use crystal walls, Great Thunderbugs and Sporepuffs in the arena.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Dragon is the only elemental weakness. In base form the chainblades take no elemental damage — physical only; that reverses while it is overloaded. All status effects rate 2-star.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head, left and right chainblades, tail (severable).

Recommended Skills & Items

Dragon weapon, full healing stock, Demondrug/Armorskin, Weakness Exploit.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Arch-Tempered Arkveld added in Ver. 1.041 (17–18 Feb 2026) as permanent event quest "Freedom from Solitude" (HR 100+), with γ armour; its 10★ Challenge Quest "The Proud Child of the Wilds" ran 18 Mar – 1 Apr 2026.
