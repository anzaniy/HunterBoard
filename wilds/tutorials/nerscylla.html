MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Nerscylla

Oilwell Basin · Iceshard Cliffs · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

Nerscylla is a status-pressure Temnoceran rather than a raw-damage check. It poisons with its retractable mandibles and back spikes, puts hunters to sleep with its stinger, and pins them with web shots, so the fight is largely about ailment preparation and reading long wind-ups. Its Gypceros hide changes its elemental profile once broken, which makes part damage part of the damage plan, not just a reward chase.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • The Gypceros hide grants Thunder immunity and a Fire weakness; after the hide/mantle is broken the body becomes weak to Thunder and much more resistant to Fire.
  • Back spikes can be broken twice, and each break removes a poison source.
  • It inflicts Poison, Sleep and Webbed; webbing roots the hunter in place.
  • In its Area 4 nest it clings to ceiling webbing and can dig beneath the floor webbing to ambush from below.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Pincer attack: mandibles extend to several times their resting length; long wind-up, heavy poison payoff.
  • Web shooting: aims its abdomen and fires three projectiles, and can repeat the volley twice in a row.
  • Stinger stab: applies Sleep; the wind-up resembles the web shot, but the claws stay still instead of flailing.
  • Ceiling swing: swings down with its poison back spikes extended.
  • Enraged dash: rears back, fires a web line, then charges; can chain several times and can start from the ceiling.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • After the pincer attack the mandibles stay exposed; a Focus Strike there makes it reel back and briefly topple, with an internal cooldown on the interaction.
  • Dodge web volleys sideways instead of backing up; the projectiles are easy to outrun laterally.
  • Slinger hits can interrupt the ceiling swing and stagger it.
  • Break the mantle early to flip it from Fire-vulnerable to Thunder-vulnerable.
  • During the underground pincer it tracks a stationary hunter, so move late and then dodge.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fire while the hide is intact; Thunder after the hide breaks. Resists Water and Dragon. Inflicts Poison, Sleep and Webbed.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Back spikes (twice; stops the poison), mantle/hide, left claw, and stinger.

Recommended Skills & Items

Antidote or Herbal Medicine, Energy Drink, Cleanser, plus Poison Resistance, Sleep Resistance and Bind Resistance as counters. Match weapon element to the hide state.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

A "Nerscylla Clone" is fought in the Omega Planetes crossover content added with Title Update 3 (released 2025-09-29); no balance changes to the field monster were found.
