MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Gypceros

Windward Plains · Oilwell Basin · Iceshard Cliffs · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · MEDIUM confidence

A Bird Wyvern that fights dirty — it flashes, spits poison, and plays dead. It is an optional High Rank target unlocked in Chapter 4 after "New Ecosystems", with the 4-star optional quest "Mysterious Poison".

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Fakes death: it collapses and lies still, then flashes or attacks when you approach to carve. Do not walk in without a plan.
  • Crest flash blinds hunters in a wide cone.
  • Poison spit leaves lingering puddles; Puddle Pods can clear them.
  • Shock Traps do not work on it; Pitfall Traps do.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Flash — the crest lights up before the burst; turn away or block.
  • Poison spit and the puddles it leaves, which punish standing still.
  • Tail whip and a running peck/charge.
  • The fake-death wake-up, which is the classic cart.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Break the head twice — a double head break permanently stops the flash, which is the single biggest quality-of-life improvement in the fight.
  • Punish after the flash (it recovers slowly) and after the poison spit.
  • Fire is the best element and the head is the place to apply it.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fire is the best element, peaking at 6 on the head. Full elemental table not captured for this entry.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head only.

Recommended Skills & Items

Puddle Pods, Pitfall Trap, Flash resistance via gear or careful positioning, healing.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Tempered and Frenzied variants both exist.
