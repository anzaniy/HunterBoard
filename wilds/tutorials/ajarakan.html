MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Ajarakan

Oilwell Basin · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

Ajarakan is a Fanged Beast that fights with heavy forelimb blows and an escalating heat state. As it heats up it starts flinging blast-inflicting molten debris, then ignites its back, which adds passive proximity damage and blunts weak ammo. The fight is a damage race against that heat gauge: enough damage forcibly ends the state and topples it.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Body heat builds through the fight, ending in an ignited state with passive damage to anyone standing too close.
  • While ignited, weaker arrows and Bowgun ammo fail to deal significant damage.
  • It exits the state after its spinning ball slam, but not reliably; enough damage forces it out and topples it.
  • It can hang and swing from overhead debris, then leap down to slam; it can be knocked down while climbing.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Rolling Eruption: punches the ground, spins rapidly, jumps and slams for a massive AoE explosion; it then stays stuck briefly.
  • Lava Charge: while ignited, slaps its back twice with its tail before charging and releasing lava in front.
  • Ground Pound: raises both arms and slams; while ignited it leaves lava on the ground. A Perfect Block can start a Power Clash.
  • Shoulder charge: drives a shoulder into the ground and charges, throwing sparks or magma.
  • Grab: grips with both hands and slams the hunter down; mash the knife or use Slinger ammo to escape.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • After its big explosion while heated, its arms are exposed as a Focus Strike weak point and it can be knocked down.
  • After Rolling Eruption it is stuck in the ground, so close in immediately.
  • Lure it to Area 4 of Oilwell Basin and use the Ancient Forge via the Slinger for heavy damage.
  • Use Chillmantle Bugs to offset the arena heat and prevent chip health loss.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Water and Ice; resists Fire. Inflicts Fireblight and Blastblight.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

The forelegs are the main break/weak-point target, exposed after its heated explosion. No further breakable-part list was found in the sources checked.

Recommended Skills & Items

Nulberry (Fireblight), Deodorant (Blastblight), Chillmantle, traps and environmental items.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

None. Tempered and Frenzied variants exist as standard states.
