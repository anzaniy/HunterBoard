MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Mizutsune

Scarlet Forest · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · MEDIUM-HIGH confidence

A Leviathan that fights on a layer of its own foam, sliding around at high speed while seeding the arena with bubbles. It is less about raw damage and more about losing control of your movement. Head breaks escalate it into a Soulseer-style fire phase.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Bubbleblight in two tiers: small bubbles = Minor (slippery, reduced friction), large bubbles = Major (worse). Bubbly Dance converts Minor into an evasion buff.
  • Green bubbles heal hunters, red bubbles grant an attack boost — grab them.
  • It slides on foam; hitting it while sliding topples it.
  • Breaking the head twice unlocks fire-element bubbles and, at low health, a blue-glowing-eye enraged mode with Soulseer-like attacks.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Three tail attacks: uppercut slap, downward slam, 360° spin.
  • Water jet/beam; head rises and tilts toward the ground before firing — dodge on the tilt.
  • Spinning leap/reposition that showers bubbles on its old position.
  • Bubble whirlpool that spirals bubbles outward.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • After a water burst its mouth is exposed — Focus Strike there topples it (cooldown-gated).
  • Fight it near walls to cut the slide distance; punish the post-spin recovery.
  • Tail break first reduces the spin's reach while you learn patterns.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Thunder, Dragon and Ice. Resists Water and Fire.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head (twice, which triggers the fire phase), tail (severable).

Recommended Skills & Items

Bubbly Dance, Thunder weapon, Constitution/stamina skills for slide chasing.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Title Update 1 (3–4 Apr 2025), unlock at HR 21; Tempered from HR 41+; 9★ at HR 100. No Arch-Tempered form.
