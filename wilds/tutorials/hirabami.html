MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Hirabami

Iceshard Cliffs · Ruins of Wyveria · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

Hirabami is an airborne leviathan that uses the membrane on its neck to float and rain ice projectiles that freeze hunters in place. The pack element matters: three at once turn a readable one-on-one into a crowd-control problem. Breaking the head and tail is unusually valuable because it directly shortens the monster's airtime.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Floats using its neck membrane, attacking with a claw-like tail and ice shots that inflict a freeze/bind state.
  • Breaking the head makes it take longer to get back into the air.
  • Destroying a wound on the head or tail instantly topples it.
  • The tail can be broken up to five times, dropping a Heavy Slicing Pod each time; hitting the head with that pod knocks it out of the air.
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Ice projectile spit: rears its head back first and can fire one or two shots; a hit freezes the hunter for a follow-up.
  • Dive attack: loops in the air, then dives while spinning like a blade.
  • Spin smash: spins rapidly in the air before slamming down with long reach.
  • Side roll: when enraged on the ground, coils inward like an accordion before rolling sideways.
  • Tail smash into swipe: positions its tail above the hunter, smashes down, then sweeps sideways.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • After it spits ice, its mouth is briefly a weak point; a Focus Strike there knocks it down, with a cooldown.
  • Flash Pods blind it and drop it out of the air when it is hovering.
  • Large Dung Pods split the pack so you can fight one at a time.
  • Use Iceshard Cliffs ice spikes, brittle ice pillars and floating rubble traps via the Slinger.
  • If frozen, use a Cleanser immediately or mash movement to break free.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fire and Thunder; resists Ice. Inflicts Frostblight and a freeze/bind effect.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head and tail; the tail can be broken five times. Head and tail wounds topple it when destroyed.

Recommended Skills & Items

Large Dung Pods, Flash Pods, Cleanser, Heavy Slicing Pods, Bind Resistance.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

None. Tempered and Frenzied variants exist as standard endgame states, not Title Update additions.
