MONSTER TUTORIAL · LARGE MONSTER

Lagiacrus

Scarlet Forest · Wounded Hollow

01

FIGHT OVERVIEW

Community-compiled · HIGH confidence

A Leviathan that stores electricity in spinal organs and discharges it in wide arcs. The hunt's identity is its low-health underwater sequence, which is a scripted damage race rather than normal combat.

02

KEY MECHANICS

  • Charges electricity; in the charged state attacks gain Thunder and can paralyse, setting up combos.
  • Breaking the back (shockspike) shortens how long it stays charged.
  • At low health it dives into area 19: you can only move, evade and use the slinger — latch on with the hook slinger and attack with weapon/knife, fire Helix Piercing Pods, or drop submerged rubble for big damage.
  • Enough underwater damage triggers a finisher that drags it ashore; near the spiked ruins it is thrown into them for bonus damage and a guaranteed topple.
  • Fail the damage check and it creates a whirlpool then a massive area-wide discharge (effectively a wipe).
03

DANGEROUS ATTACKS & TELLS

  • Discharge: three-beat tell — neck turn, spine glow, then the burst; run out, or swim straight up if submerged.
  • Charged spinning charge with a long charge-up; second pass carries lightning.
  • Double charge when enraged (getting clipped by the first often means eating the second).
  • Underwater tackle — evade vertically.
04

PUNISH WINDOWS & STRATEGY

  • Fight around the hind legs; most attacks telegraph for a long time.
  • After a discharge the back is exposed for a Focus Strike topple; after a breath the mouth is weak.
  • Both trap types work on land; use the arena rubble at the start of the underwater phase.
05

WEAKNESSES, PARTS & PREP

Element & Status Weaknesses

Fire primary; Ice and Dragon secondary. Water and Thunder are null.

Breakable Parts / Wounds

Head/horns, back, forelegs, tail (severable).

Recommended Skills & Items

Thunder Resistance (Thunderblight leads to paralysis combos), Fire weapon, traps, slinger ammo awareness.

06

UPDATE & VARIANT NOTES

Title Update 2 (30 Jun 2025), HR 31 after "A World Turned Upside Down" + "Forest Doshaguma"; Tempered HR 41+; Wounded Hollow spawns from TU3 at HR 100. No Arch-Tempered form.
