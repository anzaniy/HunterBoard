PRIVACY · EFFECTIVE 1 NOVEMBER 2026

You are not
the product.

Hunter Board has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. This page explains the few places where data still moves, and why.

The short version

  • No sign-up, no login, no profile, no comment system.
  • No cookies and no localStorage — the site stores nothing in your browser.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or session recording.
  • No third-party advertising or ad networks are loaded.
  • Only two external requests happen: Google Fonts, and the links you click yourself.

What the site itself collects

Nothing. Quest filters, sorting, pagination and build selection run entirely in your browser. Your HRP, Zenny and quest inputs are never transmitted, and they disappear when you close the tab.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served by Cloudflare as a content delivery network and host. Like any web server, Cloudflare processes technical request data — IP address, requested URL, user agent, referrer, timestamp and approximate region — in order to deliver the page, cache it, and block abuse.

This data is generated by your browser as part of loading any website. It is not combined with anything else by Hunter Board, and there is no account to attach it to. Cloudflare's own handling is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Google Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. This means your browser contacts fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which discloses your IP address and browser user agent to Google. No cookie is required for this request. See the Google Privacy Policy.

If you prefer to avoid that request entirely, a browser extension that blocks third-party font requests will disable it; the site falls back to a system font and remains fully usable.

Links to other sites

Pages link to Kiranico, Fextralife, Game8, the official Monster Hunter site and Capcom's press releases. Once you follow one of those links, you are on someone else's site and their privacy policy applies. Hunter Board does not embed their content or receive data from them.

Email

If you email hello@hunterboard.live, the address and message are kept only for as long as it takes to handle the request, then deleted. They are never added to a mailing list, sold, or shared — except where a takedown or rights request legally requires it.

Advertising (not currently used)

There are no ads on Hunter Board today, and no ad-related cookies are set. If display advertising is introduced later, this policy will be updated before any ad code goes live, and a consent banner will appear where the law requires one (for example under GDPR in the EU/UK). Advertising would not change the fact that the tools themselves stay free.

Children

The site is a general-audience game reference and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). No personal data is knowingly collected from anyone, so there is nothing to delete — but if you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and it will be removed.

Your rights

Because Hunter Board does not build profiles or store personal data, there is usually nothing to export or erase. If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, say so and it will be removed. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to processing or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes — such as adding advertising or analytics — will be stated plainly rather than buried.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@hunterboard.live, or use the contact page.

Scope: this policy covers hunterboard.live only. It does not cover the games themselves, the App Store, Google Play, or any site linked from here.
