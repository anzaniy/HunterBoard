ABOUT THE DATA

Where the numbers come from, and where they stop.

Wilds quest values are cross-checked against Kiranico, the in-game quest screen, Fextralife and the official update notes, and are tagged to game version 1.040. Builds follow Game8's Title Update 4 index. Outlanders launch facts come from the official site and Capcom's press release, while the monster board follows the Monster Hunter Wiki roster, cross-checked against Game8. Community counts are labelled as such.

Nothing here reads your game account. There is no login, no API and no saved profile — every number on the Wilds board is entered by hand, and the site stores nothing in your browser.
